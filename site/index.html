Doku Scan

Scan documents.
Keep them yours.

Doku Scan is a free, open-source document scanner for Android. It works fully offline: no account, no ads, no tracking.

Download Source code

Finds the page

Live edge detection outlines the document as you aim, and can capture automatically when you hold still.

Flattens and cleans it

Perspective correction plus Magic Color and black-and-white filters remove shadows and sharpen text, all on your phone.

Your library, on your device

Multi-page documents you can rename, reorder, merge and export as PDF, with optional watermark and password.

Optional Google Drive backup

Back up to a folder in your own Google Drive and restore everything after reinstalling. The app can only access files it creates there.

How Doku Scan uses your Google data

Google Drive backup is optional and off by default. If you connect it, Doku Scan asks only for the drive.file permission, uploads your scanned pages and a small list of document names and page order to a folder you name in your own Google Drive, and downloads them again when you restore. Data goes directly between your phone and Google; there are no other servers. Read the full Privacy Policy.
