How it works

Three steps from paper to PDF.

1

Aim at the page

The outline follows the page as you move the camera. Auto-capture takes the photo when the page is still.

2

Crop and clean up

Drag the corners to fit the page. The page is flattened straight on, then Enhanced or black-and-white removes shadows.

3

Save and share

Pages go into your library on the phone. Export a PDF with an optional watermark or password, then share it.

Built for everyday papers

Everything a scanner needs, nothing it doesn't.

Library on your device

Multi-page documents you can rename, reorder, merge, view full screen and delete. Everything is kept in your browser storage on the phone.

Single and batch

Scan one page at a time, or shoot a stack in batch mode while earlier pages are processed in the background.

PDF export

Choose A4, Letter or auto page size and a quality preset. Add an optional watermark or password.

Optional Google Drive backup

Back up to a folder in your own Drive and restore it after reinstalling. The app can only see files it created.

Your data

Your scans stay on your phone unless you choose otherwise.

Doku Scan has no servers. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Google Drive backup, and then pages go straight from your phone to a folder in your own Drive.

The app asks Google only for the drive.file permission, so it cannot see the rest of your Drive.
