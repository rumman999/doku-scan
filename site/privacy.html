Doku Scan

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Doku Scan is a free, open-source document scanner for Android. It is built to keep your documents on your own device. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The developer runs no servers and never receives your documents or any personal data.
  • Your scans stay on your phone unless you share them or turn on Google Drive backup.
  • Google Drive backup goes directly from your phone to your own Google Drive.

Camera

Doku Scan uses the camera only while the scanner screen is open, to photograph documents. Edge detection, cropping and image enhancement all run on your device. Camera images are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Data stored on your device

Scanned pages, thumbnails, document names and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are deleted when you delete them in the app, use Settings → Delete all documents, or uninstall the app.

Sharing and PDF export

When you export a PDF, the app creates the file on your device and opens the Android share sheet. The file goes only to the app or person you choose. Doku Scan does not see or keep a record of where it goes.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off until you connect it in Settings → Backup. When you connect:

  • Google account sign-in: you sign in with Google on your device. Doku Scan uses your Google account email only to show which account is connected.
  • Permission requested: only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets Doku Scan create files and folders in your Google Drive and access only the files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • What is uploaded: the backup folder you name, and for each document its scanned page images (JPEG) and a small doc.json file with the document's name, dates and page order.
  • Where it goes: directly from your phone to Google Drive, using Google's API. It never passes through any server run by the developer.
  • Sign-in tokens: the access token Google issues is kept only in the app's memory while it runs and is never written to storage or sent anywhere except Google.
  • Deleting: deleting a document in the app moves its backup copy to your Google Drive trash. You can delete the backup folder from Google Drive at any time. To disconnect, use Settings → Backup → Disconnect, and you can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Doku Scan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you turned on. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone, and not read by humans.

Files in Google Drive are stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Doku Scan does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Open source
