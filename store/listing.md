## Full description (max 4000)
Doku Scan turns your phone's camera into a document scanner. Point it at a page, and it finds the edges, flattens the page and cleans it up into a sharp scan you can save as a PDF.

Everything happens on your phone. There's no account to create, no ads and no tracking, and your scans never leave the device unless you share them.

SCAN
• Live edge detection finds the page in the camera preview
• Auto-capture takes the photo as soon as the page holds still
• Single mode for one page, batch mode for a whole stack

CROP AND CLEAN UP
• Drag the corners to adjust the crop, with a magnifier for precision
• Pages are straightened into a flat, top-down scan
• Black & White for crisp text, Enhanced to remove shadows, or keep the Original

ORGANISE
• Every scan is saved in a library on your phone
• Rename documents, reorder or remove pages, and add more pages later
• Merge documents together
• Search your documents by name

EXPORT AND SHARE
• Export to PDF in A4, Letter or auto page size
• Choose the image quality to balance sharpness and file size
• Add a watermark or protect the PDF with a password
• Share straight to email, chat or any app on your phone

OPTIONAL GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP
Turn on backup to keep a copy of your documents in a folder in your own Google Drive. After reinstalling or switching phones, connect the same folder to get everything back. Doku Scan can only see the files it creates. It can't see anything else in your Drive.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Free and open source

Doku Scan is open source. You can read the code and report issues at github.com/rumman999/doku-scan.
